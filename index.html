Cue Verse Attribute Index

AGG (Aggression)

Tendency to attempt highly difficult pots, power breaks, and risky splitting of clusters over tactical play.

CTR (Control)

Precision of cue ball positioning, spin application (cue top/bottom/side), and speed management between shots.

SEL (Shot Selection)

Decision-making quality, path routing optimization, and choosing the smartest tactical target on the table.

POT (Potting)

Raw pocketing accuracy on straight, cut, and long distance shots into both corner and middle pockets.

SAF (Safety)

Ability to execute snookers, tight containment shots, and leave opponents hook-blocked or without a clear angle.

TEM (Temperament)

Performance consistency under high match pressure, clearing up when on a hill-hill frame, and bounce-back resilience.
